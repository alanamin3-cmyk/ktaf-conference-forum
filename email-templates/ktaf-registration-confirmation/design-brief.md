# KTAF registration-confirmation email — design brief

- **Deliverable:** Responsive transactional registration-confirmation email
- **Use context:** Sent immediately after an attendee submits the KTAF registration form
- **Objective:** Confirm the attendee’s reserved place, repeat the submitted details clearly, and provide a trusted route for questions
- **Audience:** Healthcare professionals and specialists registering to attend KTAF
- **Channel:** HTML email with a plain-text fallback
- **Working width:** 640 px email body with a 1200 × 400 px high-density banner
- **Language:** English
- **Design mode:** Digital campaign / brand-led editorial
- **Required identity:** Kurdistan Thrombosis & Anticoagulation Forum — KTAF
- **Tagline:** Advancing Science. Improving Outcomes.
- **Personalized fields:** Full name, position, city, email address, and optional registration reference
- **Primary action:** Visit `https://ktaf.krd/`
- **Reply route:** `registration@ktaf.krd`, with forwarding to `contact@denkpharma.krd` to be verified before launch
- **Sponsor treatment:** Text-only secondary acknowledgement: Denk Pharma — Exclusive sponsor
- **Editable sources:** SVG banner and JavaScript email renderer
- **Exports:** PNG banner, sample responsive HTML, and plain-text example
- **Medical review required:** No; the email contains no treatment advice, scientific claim, or programme content

## Content constraints

- Conference date, venue, and programme remain unconfirmed and are not invented.
- The email confirms registration only; it does not promise admission criteria, accreditation, travel, accommodation, or certification.
- The official KTAF logo and vascular-flow motif remain unaltered.
